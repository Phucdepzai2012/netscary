INVESTING FOUNDATIONS

Learn the basics

Use this sandbox to understand orders, risk and how portfolios behave.

LESSON / 01

Market orders

A market buy uses your virtual cash to purchase shares at the simulator's current quote. A sell converts some of your holdings back into cash. This practice engine does not route orders to an exchange.

LESSON / 02

Unrealized profit & loss

Unrealized P/L compares the current market value of open holdings with their cost basis. It can change as the simulated quote moves, and it becomes realized when shares are sold.

LESSON / 03

Diversification

Holding different assets can reduce reliance on one company, but it does not remove risk. Watch allocation and avoid putting all virtual capital into one position just to chase a short-term move.

LESSON / 04

Candlestick charts

A candle summarizes open, high, low and close. Green candles close above their open; red candles close below. A candle is a compact view of a chosen time interval, not a prediction.

LESSON / 05

Position sizing

Before placing an order, check the order estimate, remaining cash and the amount of the portfolio that the position would represent. Small practice trades make it easier to learn from mistakes.

LESSON / 06

Keep a trade journal

Use Activity to review each simulated order. Write down why you entered, what would invalidate your idea and what you learned. A process matters more than one lucky outcome.

Important: This application is an educational paper-trading simulator. The assets and prices are illustrative and generated locally; they are not real-time exchange quotes or investment recommendations. Trading real financial products involves risk.
